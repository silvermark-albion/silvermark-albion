## 💡 What is Silvermark?

Silvermark turns the market pages you open in Albion Online into prices, flips and profit calculations.
A small **Windows agent** captures market prices on your own PC while you play, and **[silvermark.app](https://silvermark.app)** analyzes them in your browser — no spreadsheets, no copying prices by hand.

## 🧭 Tools on silvermark.app

| Tool | What it does |
|:--|:--|
| 💱 **Market Flip** | Buy low in one city, sell high in another — net profit after market tax |
| ⬆️ **Live Upgrade Opportunities** | Enchanting items that pay off right now, from the latest prices |
| 🧮 **Upgrade Calculator** | Rune, soul and relic cost against the sale price of the enchanted item |
| 🐎 **Trade Calculator** | Trade runs between cities, including profit per 100 kg carried |
| 🔨 **Crafting** | Craft cost and profit, with quality and focus from your own specializations |
| 🎓 **Your Specs** | The specialization levels the agent reads when you log in |
| 📈 **Top Traded** | The most traded items by sales volume |
| 🔍 **Price Search** | Any item, every city, with how fresh each price is |
| 🪙 **Gold** | Gold price trend |
| 💼 **Portfolio Tracker** | Your own buys and sales with realized profit |

## 🚀 Get started

1. **Sign in** at [silvermark.app](https://silvermark.app) — free, with your Google account.
2. **Download the agent** — [`Silvermark-Setup.exe`](https://github.com/silvermark-albion/silvermark-agent/releases/latest) from the latest release.
3. **Paste your agent key** — click **🔑** in the site’s top bar, copy the key into the agent, then open any market in game.

## 🔒 Privacy first

- **Private mode by default** — your scans go only to your own account, never to third parties.
- **Passive** — the agent only reads network traffic your PC already receives. It never modifies, injects into or automates the game.
- **Optional Community mode** — share market scans with the [Albion Online Data Project](https://www.albion-online-data.com/) if you want to.
